PHISHING AWARENESS — LEARNING PAGE

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social-engineering attack in which an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick a user into clicking a malicious link, opening an attachment, revealing sensitive information, or taking an unsafe action.

1. Fake / misleading message
2. Urgent or tempting request
3. Suspicious link or attachment
4. Attempt to steal data or trigger action

Common Phishing Types

Email phishing: fake messages sent to many users.
Spear phishing: targeted messages tailored to one person or group.
Smishing: phishing delivered by SMS or messaging apps.
Vishing: phishing through phone or voice calls.
Clone phishing: copying a legitimate message and replacing links/attachments.

Why It Works

Phishing often exploits urgency, fear, curiosity, authority, rewards, or familiarity. A page can look convincing even when the sender or domain is wrong.
