05 / Interpretation & handling

Read these results with their limits in view.

Analysis notes

  • These items describe different aspects of the experience. They are a draft instrument, not a demonstrated validated scale. No combined satisfaction score is calculated.
  • Self-reported satisfaction does not establish learning gains or a causal effect of the activities.
  • All statements are positively worded; agreement-response bias and voluntary participation can affect results.
  • “Complete” means all ten statements have a numeric or explicit N/A answer. An all-N/A submission can be complete while contributing no numeric responses.
  • Response totals count submissions, not verified unique participants. Duplicate retries are prevented by submission reference; distinct submissions by the same person cannot be identified.

Privacy & disclosure

  • Comments are not shown in this report or included in coded-response CSV exports. Any qualitative analysis needs separate authorized access, human review, and de-identification.
  • Coded, identity-field-free exports are not guaranteed anonymous. Exact UTC times, activity labels, small groups, and response patterns can enable re-identification. Review disclosure risk before sharing.
  • The application does not request respondent names, student IDs, or contact details. Hosting and infrastructure logging require separate review before collection.
  • Storage status will be reported by the server.
