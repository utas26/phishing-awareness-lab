Storage failures are disclosed; unsaved in-memory fallback is discarded when leaving a page so restored pages cannot resurrect cleared history; explicit reset touches only the new report key.

## Truthfulness and privacy

A displayed page is not proof of reading, understanding, a mistake, or assessment completion. Scenario findings are distinguished from recorded activity. Risk describes potential real-world consequences, not a verified vulnerability or CVSS score. The browser log is not identity-verified, tamper-proof, or server-verified, and duplicated tabs may copy it. Earlier, cleared, and other-tab actions cannot be recovered.

Optional reflections are explicitly student-authored, never invented, not persisted or uploaded, and escaped in preview/export. The UI warns against entering names, account details, passwords or other personal information. The fixed fictional demo and legacy credential-safety protections remain intact.
