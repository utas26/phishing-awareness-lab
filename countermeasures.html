How to Identify Phishing and Stay Safe

Warning Signs

• Unexpected urgency: “Act now”, “Account will be blocked”.
• Sender address does not match the claimed organization.
• Link text and actual destination domain are different.
• Misspelled or look-alike domain names.
• Unexpected attachments, login requests, or payment requests.
• Requests for passwords, OTPs, MFA codes, or sensitive data.
• Unusual grammar, formatting, branding, or greeting.

Countermeasures

• Hover over links and inspect the real destination before clicking.
• Open the official website/app directly instead of using an email link.
• Verify suspicious requests through a separate trusted channel.
• Use MFA and unique passwords.
• Keep browser, OS, email client, and security tools updated.
• Report suspicious messages to the IT/security team.
• Never share OTPs or MFA approval codes.

Quick Phishing Check

Sender: Is the sender address exactly what you expect?

Domain: Is the website domain correct, not just similar?

Context: Were you expecting this message or login request?

Pressure: Is the message trying to rush or scare you?

Request: Is it asking for credentials, money, an attachment, or a sensitive action?

Verification: Can you confirm the request independently?

If You Already Clicked

Do not continue entering information. Close the page, report the message, and use the legitimate website directly. If real credentials were entered, contact the appropriate IT/security team promptly and follow the organization's account-security procedure.
