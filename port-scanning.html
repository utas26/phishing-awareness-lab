How is the network IP calculated?

IP address AND subnet mask = network address. With mask 255.255.255.0, the first three octets stay the same and the last becomes 0:
192.0.2.50 AND 255.255.255.0 = 192.0.2.0
/24 means the first 24 bits identify the network. Devices in this lab share those bits.

Service names are common port conventions, not verified software. A real service can use a different port. Timing and responses here are simplified for learning.

Open

An application accepted the connection. A service is listening; this alone does not prove a vulnerability.

Closed

The host replied but rejected the connection. No application is listening on that port in this scenario.

Filtered

The probe was blocked or received no usable reply. The scanner cannot tell whether the port is open or closed.
