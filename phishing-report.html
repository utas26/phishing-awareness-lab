What kind of attack is this?

Phishing uses a deceptive message or website to persuade someone to disclose information or take an unsafe action. An imitation sign-in page may collect credentials when a person mistakes it for a trusted service.

Suggested observations

  • What made the message or destination suspicious?
  • How did the message use urgency, familiarity, or authority?
  • How would you verify the request through an independent channel?
  • What would you recommend to prevent or report a similar attempt?
