Instructor notes: preparing a real study
  • This original instrument is a draft for expert review, pilot testing and revision. It does not establish validity, reliability or ethics approval.
  • Before recruiting students or collecting research responses, confirm institutional ethics requirements, eligibility, voluntary grade-neutral participation, consent, researcher contact details, secure storage, authorized access, retention and withdrawal procedures.
  • Keep baseline and posttest wording stable. Repeating the same quiz can introduce practice effects. Specify outcomes, sample-size rationale and missing-data rules before the study; a comparison design is needed for causal claims.
  • A questionnaire alone cannot ensure acceptance in a Q1 journal. The research design, evidence and transparent reporting matter.
