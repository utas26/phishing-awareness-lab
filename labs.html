PRACTICAL CYBERSECURITY — SAFE AWARENESS MODULES

Real-Time Cybersecurity Awareness Modules

These modules are defensive classroom activities. They teach recognition and response without performing real attacks.

SAFE SIMULATION

QR Phishing (Quishing)

Student activity: Compare several fictional QR prompts and inspect the displayed destination before deciding whether to continue.
Learning: Verify domain, context, sender/source, and avoid entering credentials after an unexpected QR scan.
SAFE SIMULATION

MFA Fatigue

Student activity: Respond to a sequence of fictional MFA prompts and decide which should be approved or rejected.
Learning: Never approve unexpected prompts; report repeated requests and secure the account.
SAFE SIMULATION

Fake Software Update

Student activity: Compare a normal update notification with suspicious browser popups.
Learning: Use official app/OS update channels; avoid unexpected executable downloads.
SAFE SIMULATION

Malicious Attachment Recognition

Student activity: Review fictional emails and filenames without opening any real files.
Learning: Check sender, context, extension, double extensions, macros, and unexpected archives.
SAFE SIMULATION

Rogue Wi-Fi Awareness

Student activity: Choose between fictional SSIDs such as official, look-alike, and open guest networks.
Learning: Verify network names, avoid sensitive activity on unknown networks, and use trusted protections.
SAFE SIMULATION

Browser Permission Scam

Student activity: Evaluate fictional notification/camera/microphone/download prompts.
Learning: Grant permissions only when expected, necessary, and from trusted sites.
SAFE SIMULATION

Social Engineering

Student activity: Identify persuasion techniques in fictional calls, emails, and chat messages.
Learning: Recognize authority, fear, urgency, reward, curiosity, and impersonation.
SAFE SIMULATION

Password Strength

Student activity: Test invented sample passwords locally and compare length/strength.
Learning: Prefer long, unique passwords and password managers; never reuse real credentials in the lab.
SAFE SIMULATION

Suspicious URL / Domain Inspection

Student activity: Compare legitimate-looking fictional URLs and identify the true registrable domain.
Learning: Detect typosquatting, misleading subdomains, shortened links, and unexpected domains.
